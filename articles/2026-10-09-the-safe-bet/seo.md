**Meta Description（463字）**
For weeks I asked myself one question: should I build the app everyone likes, or the one I like? Building for the trend looked like the safe bet.

Then I checked what happens to developers who aren't famous. The median Steam game from 2019 earned $1,136 in its lifetime.

I'm building a time-tracking app as a pixel-art game. Copper coins fill glass jars, and the shelf holds only six.

If most apps earn little either way, what should decide which one you build?
