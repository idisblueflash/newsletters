## 正文（≤ 1000 字，纯文本，不要 Markdown 语法）

I design by what I like. Following my heart is easier than guessing the needs (functionality) of others, and it gets me to a better design.

I spent two days on a mini app. When it shaped out, I started to think about what I did right.

Vertical page alignment slows me down on the detail of the text when I started designing the daily practice in the Circle Nouns app, a simple macOS app for circling out the nouns and adverbs from a text. All the details of the ancient vertical page attract me so much.

Once everything pointed at what I like, things got simpler. I could go deeper in that direction, and a better design, one that moves me the most, came out.

I believe the vibration I have when seeing the design will vibe the same type of people like me, thus the app can pass on the same happiness.

What's your favourite thing that affects your app design?
